Platform Design Philosophy

THE 3 ONES

Core principles across all REXON products

  • ONE DATA SOURCE: A single centralized data source, connecting seamlessly with fragmented enterprise systems.
  • ONE EXPERIENCE: A unified visual experience across all devices and touchpoints (PC, Tablet, Touchscreen, LED Wall).
  • ONE VERSION OF TRUTH: An absolutely transparent, consistent version of data for all users and partners involved in the system.
